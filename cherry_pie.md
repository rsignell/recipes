# Cherry Pie
## Ingredients
### The Filling

* Frozen tart cherries: 6 cups (do not thaw beforehand). 

* Granulated sugar: 1 cup (adjust slightly depending on the acidity of the cherries)

* Cornstarch: 5 tablespoons

* Ground cardamom: 1/4 teaspoon

* Fine sea salt: 1/4 teaspoon

* Almond extract: 1/2 teaspoon

* Unsalted butter: 1 tablespoon (cold)

## The Pastry

### Pie dough: 2 standard rounds (one for the base, one for the lattice top)

## Instructions
1. Construct the Filling
Place the frozen cherries, granulated sugar, cornstarch, cardamom, and salt into a medium saucepan. Set it over medium heat.  Stir to combine.  As the cherries thaw, they will release their liquid, which will mix with the cornstarch to form a slurry. 

2. Activate the Starch
Continue cooking for 10 to 12 minutes. Stir occasionally or it will burn. The mixture must reach a simmer for the cornstarch to fully hydrate and thicken. Once the liquid turns translucent and thickens to a jam-like consistency, remove the pan from the heat.

3. Incorporate the Flavourings
Stir in the almond extract and the cold butter. The butter adds a slight richness and gives the filling a glossy finish. Allow the filling to cool to room temperature. If you put hot filling into raw pastry, the butter in the crust will melt, destroying the flaky texture.

4. Assemble the Pie
Preheat your oven to 425 F. Line a 9-inch pie dish with the bottom pastry round. Pour in the cooled cherry filling. Top with the second pastry round, either as a solid sheet with ventilation slits cut into it, or woven into a lattice. Crimp the edges to seal.

5. Bake
Brush the top pastry entirely with the egg wash and scatter the coarse sugar evenly over the surface. Place the pie on a baking sheet to catch any potential spillage. Bake at 425 F for 20 minutes to set the crust structure. Reduce the oven temperature to 350 F and bake for a further 35 to 45 minutes. You must see the filling actively bubbling through the vents; this indicates the interior has reached the necessary temperature for the starch to hold its structure once cooled.

6. Cool
Remove the pie from the oven and let it sit at room temperature for a minimum of 3 to 4 hours before slicing. Slicing too early will result in the filling collapsing.
