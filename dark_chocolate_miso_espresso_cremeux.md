# Dark Chocolate Miso & Espresso Crémeux
*Dietary Profile: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free*

This recipe produces a dense, professional-grade chocolate crémeux set in 1-inch cubes. It incorporates unsweetened cacao mass and espresso powder to heighten cocoa intensity, balanced by a subtle white miso background for savory depth.

## Ingredients

#### Cream & Custard Base
- Heavy Cream (35% fat): 185g
- Whole Milk: 185g
- Egg Yolks: 75g
- Granulated Sugar: 40g
- Instant Espresso Powder: 2g

#### Chocolate & Flavor Phase
- Dark Chocolate (70% cocoa): 130g (finely chopped)
- Unsweetened Chocolate (100% cacao mass): 20g (finely chopped)
- White Miso (Shiro Miso): 10g

## Instructions

#### 1. Liquid Infusion & Chocolate Prep
1. Combine finely chopped 70% dark chocolate (130g) and 100% cacao mass (20g) in a tall, narrow vessel suitable for an immersion blender.
2. In a medium saucepan, combine heavy cream (185g), whole milk (185g), and instant espresso powder (2g).
3. Heat over medium setting until steaming with small bubbles forming at the perimeter (175°F–185°F). Whisk to fully dissolve espresso powder.

#### 2. Custard Tempering & Cooking
1. In a separate bowl, whisk egg yolks (75g) and granulated sugar (40g) until homogeneous.
2. Slowly pour one-third of the hot cream mixture into the yolk mixture in a thin, steady stream while whisking vigorously to temper the yolks.
3. Return the yolk mixture to the saucepan with the remaining hot cream.
4. Cook over low heat, stirring continuously with a heat-resistant spatula, until the custard reaches exactly 180°F on a digital thermometer. Remove from heat immediately.

#### 3. Emulsification
1. In a small ramekin, whisk white miso (10g) with a tablespoon of hot custard until a smooth slurry forms. Stir slurry back into the main custard.
2. Pour the hot custard through a fine-mesh strainer directly over the chopped chocolate. Let sit undisturbed for 60 seconds to melt cocoa butter.
3. Submerge an immersion blender completely to the bottom of the vessel, tilting slightly to release air pockets. Blend on low speed without lifting the head until a dark, glossy, homogeneous emulsion is formed.

#### 4. Cast & Tiered Thermal Setting
1. Cast the warm crémeux into 1-inch silicone cube trays. Tap trays firmly on the counter to release trapped air bubbles.
2. **Room Temperature Rest:** Allow trays to sit on the counter for 20 minutes to reduce initial heat. Press plastic wrap directly onto the surface of each cube.
3. **Refrigeration:** Transfer to the refrigerator for a minimum of 4 hours to establish proper cocoa butter crystallization (Type V crystals).
4. **Deep Freeze:** Move trays to the freezer for at least 6 hours (or overnight) until rock solid for clean extraction.

#### 5. Unmolding & Service
1. Peel silicone away from edges and push from the bottom of each cube to extract.
2. Arrange cubes on chilled serving plates and thaw in the refrigerator for 30–45 minutes prior to service to restore a silky, pipeable texture with clean 90-degree edges.

#### Notes
| Variable | Scientific Rationale |
| :--- | :--- |
| Cacao Mass Boost | Replacing 20g of 70% chocolate with 100% cacao mass increases bitter cacao solids and cocoa butter without adding extra sugar, providing structural firmness for sharp 1-inch cubes. |
| Espresso Bridge | Coffee compounds mimic and amplify roasty notes in cacao, bridging the flavor gap between the fermented white miso and dark chocolate. |
| Tiered Cooling | Resting at room temperature prevents condensation, while a 4-hour fridge rest allows slow cocoa butter crystallization before deep freezing for mechanical unmolding. |
