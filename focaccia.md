# Ligurian-Style High-Hydration Focaccia

**Total Dough Mass:** 1189g (excluding emulsion and surface garnishes)
**Hydration (Baseline):** 75.4%
**Effective Hydration (with Emulsion):** 85.1%

## Components

### Biga (Preferment)
* 335 g bread flour
* 165 g filtered water (lukewarm)
* 1 g active dry yeast

### Autolyse
* 335 g bread flour
* 255 g filtered water (lukewarm)

### Final Dough Additions
* 85 g filtered water (lukewarm)
* 3 g active dry yeast
* 10 g Morton Kosher salt

### Salamoia (Surface Emulsion)
* 65 g filtered water (lukewarm)
* 65 g extra virgin olive oil
* 8 g fine sea salt

### Pan and Surface Dressing
* ~30 g extra virgin olive oil (for pan lubrication)
* 1-2 g Maldon salt (for surface contrast)
* 2-3 sprigs fresh rosemary (lightly massaged in oil)
* 2 cloves garlic (shaved)

## Protocol

1. **Biga Preparation (8-24 Hours Prior):** Combine the biga components in a mixing vessel. Agitate manually for 3 to 4 minutes until a stiff, shaggy dough forms. Cover the vessel and allow it to ferment at ambient temperature for 8 to 24 hours. 
2. **Autolyse Phase:** In a primary mixing bowl, combine the autolyse flour and water. Incorporate until hydration is uniform. Cover and allow the matrix to rest for 30 minutes to hydrate the starches and initiate enzymatic breakdown.
3. **Final Dough Integration:** To the autolysed dough, add the final dough additions (water, yeast, salt) and the matured biga. Squeeze and fold until fully merged. Transfer to a work surface and execute slap-and-fold kneading (the Bertinet method) for 8 to 10 minutes to develop the gluten network until the dough becomes elastic and less adhesive.
4. **Initial Bulk Proof:** Lubricate a 16x12-inch baking pan with approximately 30g of extra virgin olive oil. Transfer the dough to the centre of the pan, cover it, and allow it to proof at room temperature for 60 to 90 minutes until doubled in volume.
5. **Shaping and Emulsion Preparation:** With oiled fingertips, gently stretch the dough to occupy the corners of the pan, creating a firm seal against the pan walls. In a sealed vessel, combine the salamoia components (65g water, 65g oil, 8g fine sea salt) and agitate vigorously for 30 to 45 seconds to create a temporary mechanical suspension.
6. **Dimpling and Emulsion Application:** Aggressively dimple the dough with your fingertips. Immediately pour the opaque emulsion strictly within the central zones of the dough matrix, avoiding the outermost 2 to 3 centimetres to prevent sub-crust migration.
7. **Secondary Proof:** Cover the vessel and let it proof for an additional 60 to 90 minutes.
8. **Surface Dressing and Thermal Processing:** Preheat a convection oven to 425 F (fan engaged). Distribute the Maldon salt, oiled rosemary leaves, and shaved garlic evenly across the surface. Bake on the middle rack at 425 F for exactly 19 minutes. 
9. **Cooling:** Remove the focaccia from the pan immediately and transfer to a wire rack. Allow the internal temperature to drop and the structural matrix to solidify for a minimum of 30 to 45 minutes prior to slicing.
